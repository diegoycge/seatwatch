Claude's judgment

In cloud mode, a Claude routine judges every new candidate each hour. Watch matches always notify you, with Claude's take attached. Travel-window deals only notify you when Claude rates them good or great.

What counts as a deal

One-way miles per person at or below which an award counts as a good deal, by flight distance. Taxes above the allowance get converted to points at your cents-per-point value and added to the price.
